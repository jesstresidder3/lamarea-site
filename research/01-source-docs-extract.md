**04**

**Why the design alone won't get you there**

*The part I'd rather say now than after launch.*

You've said visually getting the site to where you want it is the key thing, and I'm confident we can do that. My honest response is that a beautiful site with the current structure underneath it would look like Aman and behave like a brochure. The brands you've named aren't only prettier — they're built as a sequence: clarity in the first screen, a pathway for each audience, proof at the moment of decision, a single frictionless ask. Take any one of those away and the visual does less than it should.

Here's the practical version. Someone lands on a redesigned La maréa homepage from Instagram. If they still see three audience cards and one generic form, the design has bought you a nicer bounce. If they see Private Retreats and Corporate Retreats as two doors, walk through the right one, read a page written for them, see a testimonial from someone like them, and fill in a form that already knows what to ask — that's an enquiry, and then a call. The design makes them stay. The structure makes them act.

*"A luxury website doesn't describe an experience. It recreates the feeling of being offered one — and then makes it effortless to say yes."*
