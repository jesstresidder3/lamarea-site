The Encounter Bay guide has no literal run sheet. This arc is assembled from the flow bullets on page 17 and the times on page 24, and it should be confirmed with Belle before it drives a website section.

7am pick up in Adelaide. 8am arrive, exclusive use of the property begins. ## W. Technical architecture decisions (added 16-09-2026)

Source: `plan/10-technical-architecture.md`, 1,009 lines, 16-09-2026.

### The decisions

Stack. Astro 5 with static output, hand-written CSS, no UI framework, deployed to Cloudflare Pages from a GitHub repository in Belle's name. Astro content collections are what turn adding a fifth venue into data entry rather than a build, which is requirement F3.

Vercel is ruled out on its own terms. Its fair use guidelines define commercial use to cover paid site work and advertising a service, so the Hobby tier breaches, and Pro is the per-seat subscription Belle is leaving Wix to escape. That reasoning satisfies P3.

Video. Cloudflare Stream at roughly $8 USD per month, about $12 AUD. That is an estimate built on a $5 minimum storage block plus 3,000 delivered minutes at $1 per 1,000, assuming 2,000 sessions a month, and there is no analytics behind that session figure. Bunny comes in around $3 but its Oceania delivery runs at $0.03 per GB on Standard rather than the $0.005 headline.

Total ongoing platform cost at launch is about $8 USD a month plus domain renewal. That is not zero, and Belle should hear the real number rather than a promise of nothing, given P3 is about escaping recurring cost.

Editing. Sveltia CMS at `/admin`, GitHub backend, Cloudflare R2 for media. It wins on native R2 integration, which matters because media handling is most of what Belle does, on a Decap-compatible config that caps the beta risk, and on having nothing to host. Decap is ruled out on CVE-2025-57520. Claude becomes the escape hatch rather than the interface.

Pricing. P1 makes self-editing core scope, so the CMS build sits inside the $2,900. The add-on is quoted once at $550 and covers training, a cheat sheet, 30 days of support and one structural change. That settles the four conflicting figures recorded at section P.

Guest records. The site owns them. Supabase is the permanent store of record, and Movement, the site and Klaviyo are all consumers of it. Movement's export and API capability is unverified, which is precisely why it cannot be the owner. That answers P9, K5 and K6.

### A correction that matters

The widely repeated claim that the small business privacy exemption ends on 10-12-2026 is wrong. The OAIC lists no end date for it. What commences that day is the automated decision-making transparency obligation.

The real exposure is different and larger. The small business exemption never covered health service providers. La maréa collects injuries and dietary needs in order to deliver practitioner-led treatments, so the site should be built as covered by the Privacy Act now rather than treated as exempt. That raises the bar on the Jotform replacement at P4 and it is the correct call regardless of the deadline.

### Top three risks

The footage may not carry a site of this class, and Belle has already said some of it is her husband experimenting. The domain registrar and login access are unconfirmed, and nothing launches without them. Scope is heavy, with more than sixty Must requirements inside four weeks at $2,900.
