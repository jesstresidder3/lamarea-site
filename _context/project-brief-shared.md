## Why she is rebuilding, in her words

"Functionally, the big driver for me is private group and corporate group retreat bookings. I want the site to really showcase our offerings through beautiful drone footage, imagery and video, and to genuinely work harder for me, more sales calls booked, more retreat bookings, and stronger lead generation overall!!"

The Wix Classic Editor cannot do the sliding and expanding animations she wants without moving up to Wix Studio. Building custom removes that ceiling and the ongoing platform cost.

## The commercial target

Primary conversion: a booked discovery call that leads to an 8 hour immersive full day retreat for a private group or a corporate group. That is the sale she wants to be making. Everything else on the site is supporting cast.

## Agreed structural direction (Jess to Belle, 09-09-2026, Belle: "just what i am after")

- The site reads as a story as you scroll, a series of moments rather than a list of inclusions.
- Two distinct paths from early on, private groups and retreats, and corporate bookings, so each audience is guided to what is relevant.
- The story unfolds through the experiences themselves, strong visual frames using her drone footage, quiet luxury feel.
- Belle's own phrasing of the arc: arrive, move, nourish, restore.
- Photography does as much of the storytelling as the copy.
- A smart enquiry funnel that asks relevant questions step by step rather than one giant form upfront.
- A simple formats menu for people who want to browse options directly.
- Overall aim: less "here is what is included", more "this is what your experience could feel like".

## Brand direction (Belle, 09-09-2026)

Her existing brand kit uses green as the main colour and she explicitly wants to move away from that as the base.
