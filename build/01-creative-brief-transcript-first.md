## A. Belle in one page

**What the site is for.** One sale: an 8 hour immersive full day retreat on the Fleurieu Peninsula for a private group or a corporate team, booked through a discovery call with Belle. "really the type of sales I wanna be making are those eight hour immersive full day retreats" and "get them to book a discovery call with me to curate their eight hour immersive retreat" (BD-57, BD-58). In writing: "more sales calls booked, more retreat bookings, and stronger lead generation overall!!" (EM-01). Everything else on the site is supporting cast.

**Who it is for.** People who have never been to South Australia, arriving from interstate and overseas, buying for a group. "my target market is interstate and what is going to draw them to book a trip to South Australia" (BD-39). "show them sort of the breathtaking reasons to come come there as a wellness destination" (BD-122). "if they've never been to South Australia" (BD-60). "my target audience are private groups and corporate groups" (BD-57).

**What La maréa is, in her words.** "It's bringing the the amazing Flourio Peninsula landscape together it's bringing specific wellness experiences together and amazing food and hospitality and curating these retreats." (BD-74). "these wellness experiences done in beautiful locations at five star luxury accommodation" (BD-113). "it combines amazing locations on the Florio Peninsula, luxury accommodation, and luxury wellness" (BD-128). Her brand line, from the Ponder Designs style guide: "luxury coastal wellness reimagined".

**The feeling on arrival.** "people need to look at the site and go, oh, wow. Like, that place looks amazing or that looks so relaxing or, you know, I need to experience that" (BD-78). "we can start to make them feel something, um, like, they wanna be there" (BD-78). "this is how we are going to make you feel" (BD-76).

**The counter-position.** "we live in such a fast paced world and modern society, I guess, promotes, um, yeah, promotes that sort of hustle culture" and "La Merare is all about slowing down and restoration recovery" (BD-80). Her guide says it in one line: "La maréa was created as an invitation to pause."

**The transformation.** "transform how they feel, how they think about lifestyle wellness, how they build habits" and "leave feeling different, lighter, calmer, empowered, educated, um, and just really rested" (BD-79). Her eight words carry it: Release, Reconnect, Restore, Realign, Educate, Empower, Ground, Nourish (BD-136). Empower is one of the eight and must never be dropped.

**The look.** "light and bright", "more summary" (BD-12), "Mediterranean chic" (BD-14), "simple, minimalistic, elegant. They have nice, small, luxury fonts", "lots of white space" (BD-10), soft sand as the base and "the sage green" for "wording and headings" (BD-11, BD-119). In writing: "Mediterranean luxury, high-end aesthetic... Think simplistic, minimalistic, lots of white space, and a chic overall design." (EM-02).

**How she will judge it.** She has already turned down a build that would not reach the look. "I just don't want to, you know, go on Wix Editor thinking it's going to achieve what I've got in my mind and... at the end of it it's like, oh, it's just still not where I wanted it to be." (AM-07). The current site is "very far off the mark in terms of where I want it to be" (AM-08). She can see small proportion faults: "I feel like you can notice it a lot like the difference." (AM-04). The visual result is pass or fail.
