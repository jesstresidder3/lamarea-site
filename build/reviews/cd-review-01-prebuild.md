# La maréa creative director review 01, pre-build

## A. Verdict

1. Yes in substance: every section Belle asked for has a home, her words set the structure, and the transcript-first brief (build/01) already corrected the worst earlier misreads (pillars, experiences as a slider, the map, the journal slider, the vertical reel).
2. No in feeling, yet: the spec makes the site too long (15 home sections, about 25 phone screens; 13 sections on the Day page describing the same day five ways) for someone who asked for "simple, minimalistic, elegant" and "not heaps wordy and busy".
3. The signature moments lean dark and lean Tailor: a 42 per cent olive scrim over the hero, a full-screen khaki Restore panel, rotated spine labels, overlapping circles, italic emphasis words and Cormorant Garamond, which is the stock serif of wellness templates and nothing like her HV Muse brand face.
4. A handful of drafted facts would mislead a visitor: a composite 8 hour day stitched from two venues, a fourth "partner" Belle has not signed, a 40 minute drive the venue itself says is 50, deposit terms that contradict her live terms, and a 2027 member login nobody promised.
5. Fix the Musts in section G before the page builders start and this is the site she described; skip them and it will look like a well-made luxury template with her logo on it, which is the "bit like eh" result she has already turned down once.

## E. What will not look or feel good enough

**E1. The hero will not be light and bright.** `tokens.css` sets `--scrim: rgb(35 32 14 / 0.42)`, a dark olive at 42 per cent, and build/04 puts white text over the video. A 42 per cent brown veil over "the beautiful green water" is the fastest way to lose "light and bright" in the first second. Fix: no full-frame scrim. On desktop, one soft gradient under the bottom third only, 22 per cent at most, with the text placed where the clip has calm sea or sky. On phones (below 700px) the video fills the top 65svh and the H1 and doors sit on `--sand-30` beneath it, Aman's pattern, so no scrim is needed at all.

**E2. The first screen carries too many words.** Eyebrow "Fleurieu Peninsula, South Australia", then an H1 that says "on the Fleurieu Peninsula" again, a sub-line, and two doors each with a 9 to 15 word descriptor. On a 390px phone that is about 450px of text over an 844px screen. Fix: drop the eyebrow, keep the H1 and two door labels ("Private groups and retreats", "Corporate teams"), move the sub-line into moment 2 and the door descriptors onto the path pages. Cap the hero line at 56px (`--fs-display: clamp(2.25rem, 4vw, 3.5rem)`); she asked for "nice, small, luxury fonts" and Aman's hero line is 31px.

**E3. The Day sequence will turn beige, then khaki.** build/04 10.5 ramps the panels through `--salt-25`, `--sand-40`, `--sand-60` (#EFE0C0, a yellow buff) and ends on a full-viewport `--sage-80` (#635B39, a khaki olive) for Restore. That is three screens of deepening beige and one flat dark field, on the one section she called "a focus". Fix: two surfaces only, `--salt-25` and `--sand-30`, and Restore as a full-bleed photograph of the venue in late light with a local gradient under its text. Let the photograph do dusk.

**E4. The Day sequence is over-engineered.** Spines, a rotated beat name per spine, a pillar icon per panel, a time per panel, a four-step colour ramp, a cross-fade and an in-panel image collapse (see C1) all run inside one pinned section. Fix: panels slide in from the right and settle, nothing moves inside a settled panel. Replace the rotated spines with a thin time rail across the top (7am, 8:15am, 10am, 12:30pm, 2pm, 4pm) with the four beat names on it. That keeps the "coming out from the the right hand side" she loved, removes The Tailor's most recognisable device, and puts her real schedule on screen.

**E5. Too much of it is The Tailor.** Centred logo with split nav, header hiding on scroll, the zoom mosaic, the wipe with rotated spines, a slider in a pane, the map with a large italic place name, image-above testimonials in alternating heights, three overlapping question circles, journal cards with filter tabs and one italic word per heading, in nearly The Tailor's scroll order. She named most of those mechanics, so they stay. The signatures that identify The Tailor on sight go: spines (E4), overlapping rings (replace, see G item 20), italic emphasis on random words (italic only on place names: Fleurieu, Deep Creek, Encounter Bay, McLaren Vale, which ties the one flourish to the one thing only La maréa has), and the 60px italic map name (40px upright).

**E6. Cormorant Garamond will make it look like a template.** It is the default free "luxury" serif on wellness themes, and it is an old-style book face. Her brand face, HV Muse (style guide page 6), is wide, low-contrast and deco, and her logo lettering sits in that family. Cormorant headings beside that logo will look mismatched to anyone with Belle's eye. Fix: license HV Muse for web now if the cost is small, or trial a stand-in with wide deco capitals for headings of 28px and up (Italiana is one candidate) side by side with the logo before the page builders start. Jost for body stays.

**E7. Nothing on the page says Mediterranean except the palette.** "building a really Mediterranean chic sort of site in and around that base and color". The style guide's page 7 shows a small arch-topped swatch under the sage ramp. An arch frame, used in three places only (the two doors, the `/places` cards, and the three customise prompts in place of Tailor rings), would carry Mediterranean architecture and replace a Tailor signature at once. It is unconfirmed as a brand shape, so it goes to Belle. Also add a grading note to the media manifest: lift shadows, warm white balance, keep the water green and turquoise, no dark vignette.

**E8. Two stacked display lines two screens apart.** "Restore. Reconnect. Realign." heads moment 2 and "Arrive. Move. Nourish. Restore." heads moment 4, both period-stacked, both with "Restore". That cadence twice in a row reads as a device. Keep hers in moment 2. Head the Day section "An 8 hour day on the Fleurieu" and put the four beat names on the time rail.

**E9. The zoom mosaic repeats the hero.** Hero: full-bleed coast from above. Two sections later: a mosaic that zooms into a second coast clip. Same feeling twice, weighted to beach, which she warned against ("it's not just showing Lamaria as, you know, all the beach stuff"). Fix: the five surrounding stills are the offer (yoga on a deck, the shared table, the plunge pool, a massage, the bath shot) and the centre frame is not beach (the left pan across the hills, X12, or a Beresford vineyard frame). Cap it at 1,000px of scroll plus a 300px hold on desktop, 700px on phones. The Tailor spends 3,300px; build/02 itself says that is too long for a site whose job is a booked call.

**E10. The map is the least luxurious moment on home, and two pins collide.** A line map on sand with pills and a small pane is the plainest moment on the page, and Beresford Estate and The Vineyard Retreat are both in Blewitt Springs, so at peninsula scale their pins sit on top of each other. Fix: the pane's venue photograph takes two thirds of the section and the map one third; group the McLaren Vale venues under one marker that opens a two-venue pane.

**E11. Components repeat until they lose their charm.** Question circles on home, private, corporate, the Day page and `/enquire`. A map on home, `/places`, every venue page, the Fleurieu page and the Day page. Fix: circles on `/enquire` (where she put them, "near the form") and as the home closing moment only; the map on home, `/places` and the Fleurieu page only.

**E12. The eight words as hover reveals.** Hidden text on hover is fiddly on touch and adds motion to the calmest page on the site. Fix: plain word and definition pairs in small type, visible.

**E13. The header is busy and then disappears.** Seven links, Menu and a filled button on a laptop, and the whole bar hides on scroll down, so for most of a long scroll there is no visible way to act. On phones the header carries "Plan your day" and the sticky bar carries it again. Fix: nav of five (The 8 hour day, Experiences, The Table, Places to Pause, Philosophy) plus Menu and "Plan your day"; the two paths live in the hero doors, the menu and the footer. The bar stays visible as a compact sand strip (Aman, her font reference, never hides its header). Phones: logo and Menu in the header, "Plan your day" in the sticky bar only.

**E14. Placeholders will fail her pass-or-fail test.** build/00 5.9 says Jess adds the media later. If Belle's first look is sand placeholders in the hero, the mosaic, the Day panels and the experience tiles, she will judge the site by boxes. Her own words: "it's going to come down to what professional imagery I have". Nothing goes to Belle until those four places carry her real stills.

## F. Walking the home page and the Day page as a first-time interstate visitor

### Home, on a phone (390 by 844), as build/04 specifies it

Screen 1 is a portrait crop of an aerial coast clip, darkened by the scrim, under roughly 450px of text and a header with a button. The "oh, wow" she asked for ("people need to look at the site and go, oh, wow") is behind a paragraph. Screen 2, the invitation to pause, is calm and good. Screens 3 and 4 are the zoom, which works if the stills are strong. Screens 5 to 10 are the Day as a vertical stack, because nothing pins on phones: a base panel and four tall panels with detail open, stepping through beige to khaki. About 25 screens in all.

The call path: the sticky "Plan your day" bar is obvious, and it doubles up with the header button. The word "call" does not appear until the end of the Day section, and the button there says "Book a discovery call" but opens a form.

### Home, on a laptop (1440 by 900)

Screen 1 has a busy top edge (seven links, Menu, a filled button) and a busy bottom edge (eyebrow, a line up to 76px, a sub-line, two doors) over a darkened clip. About 20 screens, 17,000 to 18,000px.
