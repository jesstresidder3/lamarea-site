# Belle's vision, a brief for the redesign

## Read this first

Belle wants a site that looks like a luxury travel brand and sells one product: **an 8 hour immersive day for a private group or a corporate team, booked through a discovery call with her**. Everything else on the site supports that sale.

The look she asked for is **light and bright, soft sand as the base with sage for words, small elegant type with lots of white space, big imagery first and slow motion with nothing bouncy**. She named Aman for type and calm and The Tailor for motion. COMO gave her the pillar and journal patterns, while Basq House and Bathhouse Albion gave her the rooms and experiences.

The live site has her palette and most of the sections she asked for. It falls short where she put the most weight. **The first screen** is a busy, overcast aerial under thin white type. **The imagery** across the site is often grey, dusky or missing (blank sand frames sit where photos should be). **The story** is hard to follow, because three routes lead to the same product and the home page runs to about twenty screens.

### The first ten seconds

A visitor from interstate who has never been to South Australia has to see and understand four things, in this order.

1. **This place is beautiful and bright.** Summer coast from the air, green and turquoise water, hills (T27, T55).
2. **It is luxury wellness on the Fleurieu Peninsula, South Australia.** "it combines amazing locations on the Florio Peninsula, luxury accommodation, and luxury wellness" (T60).
3. **It is for groups.** Private groups and corporate teams (T33, EM-01).
4. **The next step is a conversation with Belle.** "get them to book a discovery call with me to curate their eight hour immersive retreat" (T33).

## 4. Where the live site departs from her vision

Ranked by how much each would change Belle's first reaction. Screens are in `qa/shots/align2/`.

1. **Home hero at 1440.** The photograph is the cove aerial under a grey, overcast sky, with dark basalt cliffs and dry grass filling half the frame (`home-1440-fold.png`). The headline is thin white HV Muse at about 60px, bottom left, set directly over broken rock and surf, and it mixes roman and italic. Two door links in Cormorant with long arrows float over the rocks on the right, with a scroll line between them. No text has a calm surface behind it, so the screen reads as busy and the words are hard to read. Belle asked for summer light, "green water" and "nice, small, luxury fonts" (T27, T55). Aman and The Tailor put little or no type on the moving image. At 390 the headline sits below the image on sand and reads well (`home-390-fold.png`), which points at the fix.

2. **The imagery is rarely light or summery.** The /our-story hero is an overcast coast with dry grass. The /places hero repeats the grey-sky cove. On /retreats the hero is a low-sun beach, and its white text crosses bright sand and stones where it is hard to read. The /private-groups hero shows moiré on the window glass and a price sticker on the jug. The Places to Pause image on home is a hazy grey sea. Her rule is "light, bright, soft, coastal nature inspired tones" (Q), and the style guide's own hero is pale turquoise water from above (SG p5).

3. **Blank sand frames where photos should be.** The Encounter Bay and Beresford cards on /places, /private-groups and /corporate are empty arches with the "Lm" monogram. Three of the four practitioner portraits on /experiences are empty. /our-story has five empty frames, including every picture of Belle. Two self-led tiles (infrared sauna and pool swimming) are empty, as are several testimonial cards. Belle said "I need to show as much imagery as possible at these locations" (T30), and empty frames make a luxury site look unfinished. Hide a slot until its photo exists, and never use a Naiko photo for Beresford.

4. **Three routes to one product.** The header offers "The 8 hour day" (`/experiences/full-day-retreat`) beside "Experiences". The menu adds "Retreat formats" (`/retreats`), which opens with "Wellness retreats on the Fleurieu Peninsula" and sells half days, Wake Up To Wellness, Sunset Reset, personalised retreats and a women's weekend. A visitor cannot tell which of these is the product. Belle wants the 8 hour day to lead (T33). The header also carries five links, a Menu and a filled button, and the menu adds thirteen more.

5. **The home page is about twenty screens long at 1440 (18,257px), with twelve sections.** Belle's flow has five beats (T48). Most home sections repeat a full page elsewhere (the day, the experiences, the food, the places, the difference, the proof, the three questions, the journal, the Collective and the app). The length works against "simple, minimalistic" (T27), and the story stops reading as one arc.

6. **Mediterranean chic is thin.** The palette is correct, but every band sits in the same warm sand with headings in the same sage. There is little salt white, and little of the chic her mood board carries (white linen, lemons and olive oil, pale stone, blue water in sun). Those photos exist in her pool (the green linen table from above, the lemons, the women in white on the balcony) but sit low on the page or small.

7. **Three type families at once.** HV Muse sets the display lines, Cormorant Garamond the lead lines and door links, and Jost the body and tiny letter-spaced labels. Two serifs plus italics inside headlines is busier than the Aman type she named (T57). Several labels are very small and pale on sand.

8. **The 8 hour day is one section among many on home.** The pinned day slides in from the right as she asked (T33). It sits between a long mosaic and the experiences slider, with a lot of empty sand above and below, and does not read as the centre of the page.

9. **The discovery call is still hard to spot.** "A few short questions about your group, then a discovery call with Belle." now sits under each day in small grey text, and every button reads "Plan your day". Her own goal names the call with her (T33).

10. **There is no video.** Every hero is a still. Belle asked for drone footage on arrival and for sliders with video (T27, T53). The drone clips are not in the pool yet (question 31), so this is a media gap more than a design fault. The design should still be built for video in the hero, the pillars and at least one experience tile.

11. **Some names are the agency's, not hers.** "The Table" for food and "Retreat formats" are not her words. She called the food "signature culinary experiences" and spoke of "the flavors of the flurry" (T50, T60). Her own section words are "The Spaces" and "The Experiences" (T66).

## 5. Room for the designer

Belle has not ruled on the points below. Each is open, provided the answer stays light, soft, slow and image-first, in her palette.

1. **The hero composition.** She asked for big imagery or drone footage and a zoom on scroll. She did not ask for a headline over the image. A full-bleed image with no headline, a small line of text below the image on sand, or a small centred wordmark all fit her words.
2. **Type scale and pairing.** HV Muse is fixed for display. The body face and the label style are open. She wants small type that is easy to read, and one display face with one sans fits that.
3. **How much salt white sits against sand.** She asked for sand as the base (T27) and for white space (T27, EM-02). Bright salt bands between sand bands would add light without leaving the palette.
4. **Home length, and which sections live on other pages.** Of the experiences slider she said "whether that's on the home page. I'm not sure" (T30). She never asked for every section on home.
5. **The retreats page.** "I'm not sure if this should just be one more impactful video" (T48). The role of /retreats is open. Folding the formats under the 8 hour day or under private groups needs her yes, and a design proposal would help her decide.
6. **The map.** "whether you showed a map of, um, Australia or South Australia" and "I'm open to ideas as well" (T33).
7. **The team layout.** "maybe we find sort of a different way to set the team out" and "I'm open to suggestions" (T39, T49).
